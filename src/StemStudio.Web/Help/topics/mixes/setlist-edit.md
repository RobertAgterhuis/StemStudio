Every change is saved at once.

1. Type a new name at the top to rename the setlist.
2. **Add songs** opens your finished songs; pick one and, at Advanced level, one of its mixes.
3. **Move up** and **Move down** change the order without a mouse; you can also drag a song to its new place.
4. **Remove** takes a song out of this setlist only.

The total shows the number of songs and how long they last together.
