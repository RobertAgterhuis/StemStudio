The library keeps every audio file you saved with **Save to library** on a track page or in the mixer. Newest
items come first; the total size of all items is shown at the top.

## Using an item

- **Play** it right here, or **Download** it. The file name is the item name.
- **Rename** it (1 to 120 characters) or **Delete** it. Deleting removes the file; the track stays.
- An item shows its source track. Items stay in the library when you delete the track or a mix: they are
  independent copies.
- While an item is being prepared it shows its progress. A failed item shows the reason and can be retried.

## Sheet music

Items of the kind sheet music show a preview of the PDF (or **Open the PDF in a new tab**), the key, the tempo and
time signature, what the instrument profile can notate and any warnings. They can be downloaded as PDF, MusicXML and
MIDI. An audio item has a **Sheet music** button to notate it; see [Sheet music](help:sheet-music).

From the Advanced level you can filter by track and kind and search by name.

Saved files are for your own practice, like the imports they come from.
