A **voice profile** lets StemStudio sing a generated song in **your own voice**. It needs a short sample of you singing.

1. Press **New voice profile**.
2. Press **Record** and sing for **20 to 30 seconds**: on your own, without music in the background, in a quiet room.
   Recording stops by itself after one minute. You can also upload a recording (WAV, FLAC, MP3 or WebM, 10 to 60
   seconds). Listen to it before you save.
3. Read the [consent statement](help:voice-consent), tick it, type your name and give the profile a name.
4. Press **Save voice profile**. StemStudio checks that the sample is long enough and has enough singing in it; files
   from your StemStudio library (stems, takes, exports) are refused, because only your own recording may be used.

When the browser cannot use a microphone (or you did not allow it), the page says why; uploading still works. Each
profile shows its sample and its consent record. **Delete** removes the profile, its sample and everything derived from
it; you choose whether takes converted to this voice go too. Voice data never leaves this computer.
