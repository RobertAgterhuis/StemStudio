A setlist is a list of songs in the order you play them. Make one under **Setlists** in the side bar: press
**New setlist**, give it a name and add the songs.

- **Add to setlist** on a song page or next to a saved mix adds that song (with that mix) at the end of a setlist.
- In a setlist you can move songs up or down with the buttons, or drag them. **Remove** takes a song out of the setlist;
  the song stays in your library.
- At Advanced level each song can have its own mix and a short note ("capo 2", "start slow"), and the setlist a longer note.
- When you delete a song from your library it also leaves your setlists; the setlist tells you once.
