**Use my voice** appears on song takes that have an AI voice (not on instrumental takes or restyles). It separates the
take, converts the vocal to your [voice profile](help:voice-profiles) and mixes it back over the music, as a new take.
While the stems are prepared the row says so; the conversion itself needs the graphics card.

From the Advanced level you can shift the pitch (-12 to 12 semitones) or turn off the automatic shift to the register
of your voice.

What to expect: the result keeps the melody, timing and words of the take; it sounds most like you with a clean, dry
sample. Converted takes are labelled **AI voice: <profile name>** and are for personal use. The check against library
audio is not a voice-similarity check: use only your own voice.
