Every folder shows a picture made from the pictures of its tracks:

- A **song** folder shows the picture of its track.
- An **album** folder shows the best picture among its songs; a picture you chose for one of its tracks wins.
- An **artist** folder shows up to four album covers as a mosaic, or the one cover when it has one album.
- Without any picture the folder shows its first letter on a colour (a question mark for unknown folders).

To change a picture, open the track and choose another one there; the folders follow. StemStudio has no artist
photos. Pictures are shown in the app only: they are never part of a download, an export or a backup.
