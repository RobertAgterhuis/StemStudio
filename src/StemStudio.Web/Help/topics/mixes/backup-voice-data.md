A voice profile is a recording of someone's singing voice. In a backup it always travels **together with its consent
record**: the statement version, the person's typed name and the date. The backup details show this proof per
profile; a profile without a valid proof is never restored.

- The archive is **not encrypted**. Anyone who has the file can listen to the voice samples. Keep it on a drive only
  you can read.
- You can **leave voice profiles out** of a backup (Advanced and Expert: untick *Voice profiles*) or out of a
  restore (untick *Restore voice profiles*).
