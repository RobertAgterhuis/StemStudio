**Suggest devices** makes optional proposals to strengthen a text:

- **End rhyme** and **internal rhyme**: the rhyme partner is named and checked by the rhyme engine on this computer.
- **Assonance** and **alliteration**: repeated vowel or consonant sounds.
- **Imagery**: a metaphor or a concrete image.
- **Syllable fit** and **line rhythm**: a line closer to its section or to your rhythm target, shown as
  "11 -> 9 syllables". The counts are verified before the suggestion is shown.
- **Poetic licence**: an elision to save a syllable, an inversion to land a rhyme, an archaism, a sound spelling or slang.

Select lines first to ask only about those lines. An optional instruction ("darker", "simpler words") steers the
proposals; it may not name an artist to imitate.
