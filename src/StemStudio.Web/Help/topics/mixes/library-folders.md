Every item you save to the library is filed at once in a song folder, inside an album folder, inside an artist
folder: for example **Metallica > Master of Puppets > Battery**. Nothing has to be created by hand, and a folder
disappears when its last item is deleted.

## Where the names come from

For each level StemStudio takes the first name it has: a name you set with **Edit library info**, the band info
found for the track, the names the import reported (YouTube, Spotify or the file), or a fallback. Names that only
differ in capitals, accents, spaces, quotes or a leading "The" share one folder.

- **Unknown artist** and **Unknown album** hold items whose artist or album is not known. They come last.
- **My songs > Songs from my lyrics** holds songs made from your own lyrics.
- Items keep their place when you delete their track.

## Browsing

Artists are sorted A to Z (a leading "The" is ignored), albums by year, songs A to Z. The path at the top
("Library > Artist > Album") takes you back one or more levels. Switch between **Grid** and **List**; the choice
is remembered on this device. Inside a song folder you play, download, rename and delete items as before, and open
the track or its mixer.

From the Advanced level you can change the order (name, year, recently added, size or number of items) and open
**All items**, the flat list with its filters.

The folders exist in StemStudio only: nothing is written to folders on your disk, and pictures are shown in the app
only. Saved files are for your own practice, like the imports they come from.
