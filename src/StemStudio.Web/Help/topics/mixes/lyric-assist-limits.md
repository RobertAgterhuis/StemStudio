- **Own lyrics only.** The assistant works on your lyrics sheets, never on lyrics downloaded for imported tracks. A
  sheet that largely matches the lyrics of a track in your library is refused. If it really is your own work (for
  example a song of your band that you also imported), choose **This is my own text**, type your name and tick the
  box. The confirmation covers only that track, is shown on the sheet and can be revoked.
- **No copies.** A proposal that repeats 7 or more words in a row, or a whole line, of lyrics in your library is
  withheld; the pane counts it. Lyrics that are not in your library cannot be detected.
- **No imitation.** Instructions that name an artist or ask to write like someone are refused, also with an own-work
  confirmation.
- **Quality.** The local model is weaker in Dutch than in English; the pane shows a note when the quality is
  limited. Always read a suggestion before you accept it.
