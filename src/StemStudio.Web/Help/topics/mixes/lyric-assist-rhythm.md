**Syllables** (Advanced) shows the syllable count of every line next to the text. It is counted on this computer
after you stop typing for a moment and sends nothing anywhere. A line is marked when it is more than 2 syllables away
from the middle of its section or from the target.

The **rhythm target** turns tempo into a syllable goal per line: BPM (40-220), beats per bar (2-7), bars per line
(1-8) and syllables per beat (0.5-4). Example: 4 beats x 2 bars x 1.5 syllables = 12 syllables per line. Device
suggestions aim at the target.

**Density** is the number of words per second at the target duration. Above 4 words per second singing gets hard.

The **rhyme finder** looks up perfect and near rhymes for the word at the cursor, from local data only, with their
syllable counts; **Insert** puts a rhyme at the cursor.
