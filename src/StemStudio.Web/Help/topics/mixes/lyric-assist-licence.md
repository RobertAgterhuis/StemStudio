Lyrics may break the rules on purpose. Elisions ('k, 't, gonna), inversions for a rhyme, archaisms, sound spellings,
slang and a line in the other language are **never reported as errors**.

**Sensitivity** (Advanced) sets how much the check reports:

- **Loose**: only clear mistakes.
- **Balanced** (default): mistakes and improvements.
- **Strict**: also notes the licences it recognised, as information you can keep.

**Mark as intentional** on a correction tells every later check to leave that word, phrase or whole line alone for
as long as it is in this sheet. The list of marks (Advanced) shows them; remove one to have it checked again. With a
text selection, **Mark selection as intentional** does the same without a suggestion.

**Add to my words** (spelling suggestions, Advanced) accepts a word, such as a name, in all your sheets of that
language. Manage the list under **My words**.
