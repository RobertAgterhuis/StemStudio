**Library care** keeps your library safe and tidy. It has two tabs:

- **Backups**: write your whole library (tracks, stems, mixes, library items, sheet music, generations, voice
  profiles) into one archive file in the backups folder, download it, check it, and restore it later or on another
  computer. See [what a backup contains](help:backup-contents) and [where backups are kept](help:backup-folder).
- **Storage**: see how much space each part of StemStudio uses and how much is free, and remove files StemStudio can
  make again.

**One job at a time.** A backup, a check or a restore is a *library job*. Only one runs at a time; its progress is
shown on this page and as a small indicator at the top of every screen, so you can keep working. While a backup runs
you can still play, mix and export; only deleting things waits until it has finished.

While a restore switches to the restored library, StemStudio pauses for a few seconds and shows "Switching to the
restored library". The message disappears by itself.
