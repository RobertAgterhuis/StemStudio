**Play** opens the first song of the setlist in the mixer, with the mix you chose for it. The bar above the mixer shows
where you are ("song 2 of 9") and the note of the song.

- **Next song** and **Previous song**, or Ctrl+Right and Ctrl+Left (Cmd on a Mac), move through the setlist. With the pedal
  preset "Next / previous song" your feet do it.
- When you changed the mix of a song and did not save it, StemStudio asks before it moves on.
- **Exit setlist** goes back to the setlist.
