The search box on every library page filters while you type: results replace the folders a moment after the
last key, grouped as **Artists**, **Albums**, **Songs** and **Items**, each with its count. Press **Enter** to
search at once, **Escape** or the clear button to go back to the folder you were in. Press **/** on a library page
to jump to the box, and **Arrow Down** to move to the first result.

## What the search looks at

- Artist, album and song names, and the name of each item.
- Stems and instruments: "vocals", "drums", "bass", "guitar", "piano", "other" and the detail stems such as
  "snare", "hi-hat", "lead vocals", "strings" or "acoustic guitar", plus everyday words such as "voice" or
  "keyboard". Mixes such as "What I hear" are found by the stems they contain.

Capitals and accents do not matter ("motorhead" finds "Motörhead"). With several words, every word must be found
("metallica bass"). Matched parts are underlined in bold. Each result shows its path; the item actions work in the
results.

From the Advanced level, **Only in this folder** limits the search to the folder you have open.
