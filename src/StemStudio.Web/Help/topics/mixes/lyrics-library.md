**Lyrics** keeps the lyrics you saved on the song page or wrote here with **New lyrics**: a title, the language and the
text ([format](help:lyrics-format)).

- **New song** opens the song page with these lyrics.
- **Edit** changes the title, language or text. Songs you made earlier keep the lyrics they were made from.
- **Delete** removes the lyrics after you confirm; songs made from them keep their own copy.

Your lyrics stay on this computer.
