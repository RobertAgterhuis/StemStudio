The assistant lives in the lyrics editor: open a sheet from **Lyrics** by its title, then press **Assistant**. It works
on your **own lyrics sheets** in **Dutch or English** and does nothing until you press a button: nothing is checked
while you type.

- **Check** looks for spelling, grammar and flow (awkward phrasing, stumbling stress, repeated words). Spelling comes
  from a dictionary on this computer and appears at once; grammar and flow come from the AI model and stream in.
- **Suggest devices** proposes rhymes, internal rhyme, assonance, alliteration, images, a better syllable fit and
  deliberate poetic licences.
- **Alternatives** writes 1 to 5 versions of the line at the cursor, **Continue** proposes lines after the block at
  the cursor and **Title ideas** proposes titles (Advanced).

Every result is a **suggestion**: a highlight in the text and an entry in the list with its category and a short
explanation in the language of the lyrics. Your text only changes when you **Accept** (or **Accept all** for the
current filter). Each accept saves a new revision and can be undone with **Undo**. Lines that came from a suggestion
get a small marker. Unsaved changes are saved first.

Keys: **Ctrl+Alt+C** checks, **F8** and **Shift+F8** move between suggestions, **Alt+Enter** accepts,
**Alt+Backspace** rejects, **Alt+I** marks as intentional and **Escape** returns to the text.

Expert shows the revision list, where you can restore an earlier text as a new revision.
