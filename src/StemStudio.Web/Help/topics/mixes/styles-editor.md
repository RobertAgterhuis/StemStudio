An **own style** describes the music you want:

- **Name** (1 to 60 characters, unique in the library), **genre** and an optional **sub-genre**;
- **Tempo feel**: follow the source, laid back, driving, half time or swing;
- **Instruments** and **moods**: up to 8 tags each; type a tag and press Enter;
- **Vocals**: keep them, or make the style instrumental (the vocal stem is left out when you restyle);
- **Default strength**: where the [strength](help:restyle-strength) slider starts;
- **Description** (up to 300 characters): part of the instructions for the model.

**No imitation.** StemStudio refuses texts that name an artist, a band or a song, or that ask to sound like someone
("in the style of", "sounds like"), also artist names from your own library. The field and the reason are shown; describe
the music instead. The check cannot know every name, so the rule is yours to keep too.

Takes you made earlier keep a copy of the style as it was, so changing or deleting a style never changes them.
