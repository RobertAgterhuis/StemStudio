**New song** makes a song from **your own lyrics**: music in a style you choose, sung by an AI voice.

1. Type or paste the lyrics, upload a `.txt`, `.lrc` or `.md` file, or pick lyrics you saved earlier
   ([format](help:lyrics-format)). **Add sections** proposes verse and chorus markers ([sections](help:lyrics-sections)).
2. Give the song a title and choose the [language](help:song-language) of the lyrics.
3. Choose a **style** from the style library, a **lead voice** (female, male or any) and the **length** (10 seconds
   to 8 minutes; 2 minutes by default). **Instrumental** makes the backing without vocals.
4. From the Advanced level you can also write a **free description** instead of a style, add **delivery** words
   ("breathy, soft"), fix the **tempo**, **key** and **beats per bar**, and make **two takes**.
5. Press **Generate**. The song appears under **Your songs** with its progress; when it is done it plays there, is saved
   to your library and can be imported as a track to practise with.

When there are many words for a short song, a warning says the singing may sound rushed; you can still generate.
Every take is labelled **AI voice** and **AI-generated**, and is [for personal use](help:generation-personal-use) only.
Write about anything, but never ask for the style or voice of a real artist.
