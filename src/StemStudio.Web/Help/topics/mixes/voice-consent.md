A voice profile is only for **your own voice**. Before a profile can be saved you:

- read the consent statement (in English and Dutch), which says that it is your own voice and that StemStudio may use it
  on this computer to convert generated vocals into your voice;
- tick the statement and type your name.

The statement's version, your name, the date and a fingerprint of the sample are stored with the profile and shown on
the voice profiles page. Never record or upload someone else's voice, and never a voice from a song: StemStudio refuses
audio from its own library, but it cannot recognise every recording, so this rule is yours to keep.

Everything stays on this computer. Deleting the profile deletes the sample and everything made from it.
