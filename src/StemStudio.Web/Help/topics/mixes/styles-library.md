The **style library** holds the styles you can [restyle a track](help:restyle-options) with. Each style describes
music: a genre family and sub-genre, a tempo feel, the instruments, the mood, what happens with the vocals and a
default [strength](help:restyle-strength).

- **Presets** are curated by StemStudio and cannot be changed. They never name artists, bands, songs or labels.
- **Own styles** are yours.

Filter by genre, mood, instrument or kind (presets or your own), or search by name, description or tag. The list keeps
the presets first and then sorts by name. The library also works when no graphics card is available; only restyling
itself needs one.
