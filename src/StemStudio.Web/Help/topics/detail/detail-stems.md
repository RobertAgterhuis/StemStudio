Detail stems are a second, optional separation. Drums can become kick, snare, toms, hi-hat and cymbals;
vocals lead and backing; other instruments, guitar and piano several instrument groups. What is left over
goes to "rest".

The detail phase starts by itself after the standard separation, or with **Split further**. It takes much
longer. In the mixer a split stem gets an **Expand** button.
