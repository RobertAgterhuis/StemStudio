title: "Cancel or retry detail stems"
area: detail
summary: "Cancel stops a queued or running detail phase; Retry starts a failed or cancelled one again."
anchors: ["detail-cancel-retry"]
keywords: ["cancel", "stop", "retry"]
related: [detail-stems, track-delete]
reviewed: "004"
---

- **Cancel detail separation** stops the detail phase. The standard stems are not affected.
- **Retry detail separation** starts a failed or cancelled phase again.
- **Split further** starts the phase when it never ran.
