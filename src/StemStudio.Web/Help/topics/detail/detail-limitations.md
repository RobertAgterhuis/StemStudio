Detail separation is experimental. Quality varies per song and instrument, and some instruments cannot be
separated: bass is never split, organ is part of keys, and instruments that blend together may stay in "rest".
Only detail stems that are audible in this song are offered.

The detail phase takes considerably longer than the standard separation. The standard stems stay playable
in the meantime.
