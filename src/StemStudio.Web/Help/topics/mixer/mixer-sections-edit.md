Press **Edit sections** below the section lane.

- **Split at the playhead** cuts the section you are in where the playback is. The cut snaps to the nearest bar line
  (or beat) when the tempo grid allows it.
- **Merge with the next** joins two sections; the first one keeps its name and kind.
- Type a **name**, or change the **kind**. For a solo you can say which instrument plays it.
- **Starts at** moves the boundary with the section before it. Use the arrow keys for small steps. Every section stays
  at least one second long.
- **Delete** gives the time to the section before it. **Add a section** fills the rest of the song.

Names such as "Verse 2" are renumbered when you add or remove a verse; names you typed yourself stay as they are.

**Done** saves all changes at once, **Cancel** throws them away. When another window saved the sections first, StemStudio
tells you; open the editor again to see the newest version. Your edits stay until you detect the sections again.
