StemStudio finds the parts of the song for you: intro, verse, chorus, solo and so on. They appear as a row of buttons
above the mixer panels. Finding them takes a few seconds the first time you open a song.

- **Click a section** to loop it. The loop goes on and the playback jumps into the section when it was somewhere else.
- **Loop this section** (Shift+L) loops the part that is playing now.
- **Next section** (N) and **Previous section** (Shift+N) jump to the neighbouring part. While a section loop is on,
  they move the loop to the next or previous part, so you can practise the song part by part.
- **Section start** (Backspace) goes back to the start of the part that is playing.

The looped section is also the region of the speed trainer. When you move marker A or B by hand, the loop no longer
belongs to the section.

The labels are a guess from the music. When the tempo grid is uncertain the lane says so, because the boundaries may
then be a little off.
