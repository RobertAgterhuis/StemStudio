- An electronic kit: its stereo output into two interface inputs, **stereo**.
- An acoustic kit: one overhead microphone (mono) or a stereo pair. Browsers record at most two channels.
- Turn the gain down: drum hits are loud and clip easily. Choose part **Drums**.
