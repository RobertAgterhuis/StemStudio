When StemStudio has timed lyrics for a song, the mixer shows them in a banner at the bottom of the screen, just above
the transport. You see the previous, the current and the next line; sung words change colour and a ball lands on each
word when it starts. Play, pause, seek and the A-B loop are followed exactly.

- Before the first line, and in instrumental gaps longer than 5 seconds, a **countdown** of three dots shows when the
  singing starts again.
- **Lyrics** in the banner bar shows or hides the banner; StemStudio remembers your choice in this browser.
- With reduced motion (system setting or Preferences) the ball does not bounce; the current word is underlined.
- **Show full lyrics** lists every line as plain text, for reading ahead or with a screen reader: the moving banner
  itself is not announced.
- From the Advanced level you can choose the text **size** and correct the timing with the
  [offset](help:karaoke-offset). Expert also shows where the timing comes from.

Without timed lyrics the banner explains why (still searching, instrumental, not found, being aligned) and links to
the track page, where you can [search again or enter the lyrics yourself](help:lyrics-sources).

Lyrics are copyrighted. They are shown for your personal practice only and are never exported, saved in mixes or shared.
