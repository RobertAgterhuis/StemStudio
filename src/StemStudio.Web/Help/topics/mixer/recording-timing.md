At Expert the take strip has a **timing** slider (-200 to +200 ms in 0.1 ms steps). You hear the shift at once;
**Apply** stores the take again with the new timing, **Reset** goes back. A positive value moves the take later.

The take keeps playing from its old audio until the new one is saved. From Advanced the strip shows "Timing adjusted by X
ms" for a stored adjustment. Prefer [calibrating the latency](help:recording-latency) for a constant offset.
