**Monitoring** plays your input on the output while you record. It is off by default.

- Use **headphones**: with speakers the input feeds back and is recorded too.
- The monitored sound arrives a little late (the round-trip latency). Direct monitoring on your interface or amp has no
  delay and is better where you have it.
- From Advanced you set the monitoring volume. Monitoring is never part of a take or an export.
