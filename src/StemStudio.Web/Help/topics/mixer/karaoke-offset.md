If the words light up too early or too late, drag the **Offset** slider in the banner bar. The change applies at once
and is saved for this song a moment after you stop.

- Positive values make the lyrics come **later**, negative values **earlier**. The range is -5000 to +5000 ms in steps
  of 10 ms.
- With the keyboard, the arrow keys move the offset by 50 ms.
- **Reset** sets it back to 0 ms.

Lyrics that are timed per line only have estimated word times: an offset fixes a constant delay, not single words.
