Press **Score** (or F) in the mixer to show the sheet music of this song. Choose one in the list when you made more than
one, for example the bass and the guitar part. Sheet music you make on the song page appears here; it takes a moment
before a new score can follow the playback.

- The bar you hear is outlined and hatched, at every speed, in loops and after a seek.
- The page scrolls along. Scroll yourself and it waits 4 seconds; press **Follow** (Shift+F) to switch scrolling off or on.
  With reduced motion the page jumps instead of scrolling smoothly.
- **Click a bar** to play from there. At Advanced level **Shift+click** loops the bars from the loop start (or just that
  bar) to the bar you clicked.
- While the song is transposed, the notes stay in the original key.
- A score made with a tempo you typed can drift from the music; the panel says so.
- **Read bar** tells a screen reader which bar is playing.
