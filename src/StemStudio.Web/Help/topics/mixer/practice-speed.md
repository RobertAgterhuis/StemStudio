Open the **Practice tempo** panel with its title, then drag the **Speed** slider, or press **-5 %** and **+5 %**. Every stem, detail stems included, plays slower or faster
together and keeps its pitch, so you can learn a fast part note by note. The click, the karaoke ball and the position
follow the slower music.

- The slider goes from 50 % to 120 % in steps of 1 %. With the keyboard: arrow keys 1 %, Page Up/Down 10 %, Home 50 %,
  End 120 %. In the mixer, **-** and **=** change the speed by 5 % from anywhere outside a text field.
- **≈ BPM** shows the tempo you hear now (the song's tempo times the speed) when a tempo is known.
- **Original speed and key** (or **R**) goes back to 100 % and 0 semitones. At Advanced level **100 %** resets only the
  speed; at Expert level you can type exact values.
- The speed is saved with the mix. Exports to the library always use the original speed and key.

The panel opens by itself when the mix uses a practice setting (speed, key, count-in or speed trainer) and stays
closed otherwise, so the channel strips keep their room. Once you open or close it, this browser remembers your choice.
Closed, its title shows what is not at the original setting, for example "75 %, +2 st, count-in".

The first change away from the original speed restarts playback once at the same position (a short gap); after that,
changes are heard within a quarter of a second without stopping.
