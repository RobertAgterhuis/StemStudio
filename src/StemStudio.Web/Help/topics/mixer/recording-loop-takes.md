Set an A-B loop around a passage and enable it, then press **Record**. Every pass becomes its own take between
A and B; a last pass of at least 1 second is kept and marked **partial**. A session stops after 50 passes or 30 minutes.

From Advanced the Takes panel groups the passes of one session and shows **Pass 1**, **Pass 2** and so on. Star the best
pass and choose **Delete the other passes** to keep only that one.
