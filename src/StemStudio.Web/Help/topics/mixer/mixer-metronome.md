**Click on/off** plays a click on every beat of the detected [beat grid](help:beat-grid); the first beat of a
bar is accented. Set the click volume with the slider.

At Advanced level you can correct the grid: enter a BPM, move the click with the offset in milliseconds, set
the beats per bar, or reset to the detected values.

The [count-in](help:practice-count-in) uses the same click sound and volume, even while the click is off.
