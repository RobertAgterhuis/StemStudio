When a take is in the **Take** strip:

- **My take** plays your take and mutes the original part.
- **Original** plays the original part and mutes your take.
- **Both** plays both.

Press `C` to cycle. The switch is sample-exact and leaves your own mute and solo settings alone. A take recorded with part
**None** has only an on/off switch. The take strip (volume, pan, mute, solo) is saved with the mix.
