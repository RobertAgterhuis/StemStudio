- **M (mute)** silences a stem; the strip is dimmed.
- **S (solo)** plays only the soloed stems. Solo several stems to hear them together.

To play along on drums, mute the drums and play the rest; to learn a part, solo it. The
[practice presets](help:mixer-presets) do this in one click.
