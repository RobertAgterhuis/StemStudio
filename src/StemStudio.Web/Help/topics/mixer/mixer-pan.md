Pan places a stem in the stereo field from fully left (-100) to fully right (+100). Centre is 0. Moving a part
to one side makes it easier to hear next to the others.

Pan is available from the Advanced level. A pan set there stays applied at Beginner and is shown as a small
"Panned" note on the strip. See also [Mute and solo](help:mixer-mute-solo).
