The transport is docked at the bottom of the mixer.

- **Play** starts all stems together, perfectly in sync.
- **Pause** keeps the position; **Stop** returns to the start.
- Drag the position bar to jump. The counter shows the position and the length.

Space plays and pauses when no button or field has focus.
