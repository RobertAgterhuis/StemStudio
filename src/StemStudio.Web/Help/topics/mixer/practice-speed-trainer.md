The speed trainer turns the [loop](help:mixer-loop) into an exercise. Set A and B around the hard part, turn the loop
on and then **Speed trainer** on. Press play: pass 1 plays at the **start** speed; after the chosen number of **passes
per step** the next pass is one **step** faster, until the **target** speed. There it keeps looping.

Example: start 60 %, step 5 %, target 100 %, 2 passes per step plays twice at 60 %, twice at 65 % and so on.

- StemStudio cannot hear whether you played cleanly, so every completed pass counts. Stop whenever you like.
- The trainer needs an enabled loop of at least 0.5 s.
- While it runs the speed slider shows the trainer's speed and cannot be dragged; transpose still works.
- Pause and play keep the pass and speed; stop, a new loop or a changed setting start again at pass 1.
- The target must be above the start. The settings are saved with the mix; the progress is not.
- **T** turns the trainer on or off.
