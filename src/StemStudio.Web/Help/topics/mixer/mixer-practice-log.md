While you play a song in the mixer, StemStudio notes how long you practise: the playing time (with the count-in, without
pauses), the time per section, how often a loop went round, and the highest speed of the speed trainer that you played
a whole section at.

Press **Practice log** in the mixer to see it:

- **Today**, **Last 7 days** and **Total** for this song.
- At Advanced level a table with the time per section and the best trainer speed.
- A chart of minutes per day, and at Advanced level of the trainer speed per section. **Show as table** shows the same
  numbers as a table; a sentence below each chart says what it shows.
- **Delete the log of this song** removes its sessions.

The log is saved every 30 seconds while you play, so a crash loses at most half a minute. A pause longer than ten
minutes starts a new session.
