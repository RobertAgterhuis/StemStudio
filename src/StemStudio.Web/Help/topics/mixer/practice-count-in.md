Turn **Count-in** on and press play: you hear one bar of clicks (four in 4/4, accent on the first) and the music starts
exactly on the next beat of the song. The dots above the transport show the beats that are left.

- The clicks follow the song's beat grid, or your manual BPM, and get slower with the [speed](help:practice-speed).
- They use the click sound and volume, even while the click is off; at volume 0 the count-in is visual only.
- At Advanced level choose **1 bar** or **2 bars**, and **Before every loop pass** to get a count-in before each
  repetition of the A-B loop (the loop must be on and at least 0.5 s long).
- Pause, stop or seek during a count-in cancels it; the next play counts in again.
- The count-in needs a tempo: a reliable beat grid or a manual BPM in the click panel. Without one it is unavailable.
- **C** turns the count-in on or off. The setting is saved with the mix.
