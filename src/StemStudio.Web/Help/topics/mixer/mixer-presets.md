| Preset | What it does |
|---|---|
| Play along: drums | Mutes the drums, unmutes the rest, turns the click on |
| Play along: bass, guitar, piano | Mutes that stem and unmutes the rest |
| Karaoke | Mutes the vocals |
| Focus: vocals | Solos the vocals |
| Reset mix | Unmutes and unsolos everything, volumes 100 %, pan centre, click off |

A preset changes the mix like doing it by hand, so the mix shows unsaved changes.
