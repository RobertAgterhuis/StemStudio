In the Takes panel choose **Export** on a take:

- **Take only** saves the take itself, trimmed to where you played.
- **Take + mix** saves what you hear: the mix with your take, including the compare choice.

Formats are the same as for other exports (FLAC, WAV, MP3). The item appears in the **Library** with a name that starts
with the take's name. A take that is still being saved can be exported once it shows "Saved".
