- **Save** stores the changes in the mix you loaded, or asks for a name the first time.
- **Save as** stores the current settings as a new mix.

Names are 1 to 100 characters and unique per track. "Unsaved changes" appears after every change until you save.
Saved mixes are in the [mix library](help:mixes-library).
