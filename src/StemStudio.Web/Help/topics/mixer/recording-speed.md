Takes are recorded at the song's original speed and key, so they line up sample-exactly with the stems. When the
speed is not 100 %, the song is transposed or the speed trainer runs, **Record** is disabled with the reason;
**Set to 100 %** restores the original speed and key in one click.

Playback of takes works at every speed and transpose: a take follows the stems like any other channel.
