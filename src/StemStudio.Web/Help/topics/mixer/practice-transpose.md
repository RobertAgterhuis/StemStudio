Use **-1** and **+1**, or focus the value and press Arrow Up/Down, to shift the whole mix by one
[semitone](help:semitone) at a time, from -12 to +12. The speed stays the same, and speed and transpose combine.
In the mixer, **,** and **.** do the same from anywhere outside a text field.

A singer can move a song into her range; a saxophone or clarinet player can play along in his instrument's key; a
guitarist can match a song tuned down a semitone without retuning.

Shifted voices sound thinner going up and darker going down: there is no formant correction. The setting is saved with
the mix; exports keep the original key.
