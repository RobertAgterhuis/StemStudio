The **Stem quality** card on the track page rates every stem and every detail stem, so you know which stems you can
trust for practice. Each rating has a word and an icon, never just a colour:

- **Good**: clean enough to practise with.
- **Fair**: usable, but some sound of other instruments or some watery sound is in it.
- **Poor**: much of what you hear belongs to other instruments, or the sound is damaged.
- **Probably not in this song**: the instrument does not seem to play; what you hear is spill from other instruments.
- **Not rated**: there is too little audio in the stem to say anything (less than about 10 seconds of sound).

The ratings are **estimates** made from the stems alone, without the original recording. StemStudio has no copy of the
separate instruments the song was recorded with, so it cannot measure the truth; it looks for signs instead. A rating
can be wrong, and the card always says so.

**When reports are made**: automatically after every separation and every detail split, and for older tracks when you
press **Check stem quality**. Press **Check again** to make a new report, for example after an update: a report made
with an older version is marked "Made with an older version". The previous rating stays visible as "was Fair" when
the new one differs. Mixing, exports and imports never wait for a report.

In the mixer each channel strip shows the rating as a small badge; select it to come back to this card.
