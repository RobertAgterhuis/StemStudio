Each stem shows at most two tips, the most useful first. Some tips have a button:

- **Split further** (detail separation may pull apart the instruments mixed into this stem): starts the detail
  separation of this stem, as on the detail card.
- **Open detail stem** (use a detail stem instead, or listen to the "rest" stem too): opens the mixer with this stem's
  detail stems expanded.
- **Mute** (the instrument is probably not in this song): opens the mixer with this stem muted. The mix is not saved
  until you save it.
- *Hard to isolate*: some instruments, such as guitar and piano, are hard to separate with today's models; the stem is
  close to the best StemStudio can do. There is no button.
- *Clean enough to practise with*: nothing to do.

Why is there no "run it on the GPU" tip? The graphics card runs **the same models** as the processor and gives the same
stems, only faster. Running a separation again on the GPU would not make a stem cleaner, so StemStudio never suggests
it.
