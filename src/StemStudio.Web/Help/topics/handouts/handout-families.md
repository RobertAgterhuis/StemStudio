Every family gets the song facts (tempo, time signature, key, length, artwork), the form chart (sections with bars and
practice tempo) and your practice notes, so no instrument gets less.

**Band overview**: the lyrics with the chords above the words and the chord chart per section. No sheet music.

**Vocals**: the lyrics with the chords above the words and the vocal sheet music.

**Guitar**: the lyrics with the chords, the chord chart, the guitar tablature and the guitar sheet music.

**Bass**: the chord chart, the bass tablature and the bass sheet music. The lyrics with chords are optional.

**Keys**: the lyrics with the chords, the chord chart and the piano or keys sheet music.

**Drums**: the drum notation and the first line of each section as cues. No chords.

**Melodic instruments** (strings, brass, woodwinds): the chord chart and the part for your instrument, in C, B-flat,
E-flat or F.

From Advanced you can add optional parts, such as the tempo you reached per section in the practice log, or leave parts
out. A part that does not exist yet never stops a handout: it is left out and listed as "Not included", with a link to
make it (for example "Make tablature").
