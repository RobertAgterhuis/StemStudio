A song, its lyrics and its music belong to their rights holders. Handouts are for your own practice and your lessons:
do not publish, sell or share them beyond your lesson.

- Every page footer says "For personal practice and teaching only". The notice is fixed and cannot be removed.
- The last page has the full notice and the credits of the sources and models used, such as where the lyrics came from.
- Chords, sheet music, tablature and timings were made automatically and may contain errors. Check them before a lesson.
- The text of a handout is real, selectable text, but the sheet music pages are pictures, so a screen reader cannot read
  them. Handout PDFs are therefore not fully accessible.
