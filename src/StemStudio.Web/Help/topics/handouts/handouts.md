Open **Lesson handout** on the track page and pick a family, such as Bass or Drums. One click makes a PDF with the
song's facts, the sections with bar numbers, the chords, the family's sheet music or tablature and your practice notes.
Parts that do not exist yet are left out, and the page lists them ("Not included: tablature").

The PDF goes to the **library** as an item of the kind "Handouts": preview it, download it, rename or delete it like
any other item. **Make again** makes it anew with the song's current lyrics, chords and notes, with the same choices.
From Advanced, **Customise...** lets you choose the parts, the sheet music, the paper size and, for melodic
instruments, the transposition.

On a setlist, **Setlist handout** makes one lesson PDF for the whole set and one family: a cover, the running order
and one section per song (up to 50 songs). It is kept in the library. The other button, **PDF for the stand**, is the
quick print of the set for the music stand; it is not stored.
