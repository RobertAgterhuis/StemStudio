Open **Lesson handout** on the track page and pick a family, such as Bass or Drums. One click makes a PDF with the
song's facts, the sections with bar numbers, the chords, the family's sheet music or tablature and your practice notes.
Parts that do not exist yet are left out, and the page lists them ("Not included: tablature").

While the PDF is made, a status line under the menu shows the progress; **Open in library** takes you to it. The PDF
goes to the **library** as an item of the kind "Handouts": preview it, download it, rename or delete it like any other
item. **Make again** makes it anew with the song's current lyrics, chords and notes, with the same choices. A handout
that failed shows **Retry** in the library. At most 3 handouts of one song or setlist are made at a time; a fourth
says "wait until one is ready".
From Advanced, **Customise...** lets you choose the parts, the sheet music, the paper size and, for melodic
instruments, the transposition.

On a setlist, **Lesson handout per instrument** makes one lesson PDF for the whole set and one family: a cover, the running order
and one section per song (up to 50 songs). It is kept in the library. The other button, **Print setlist**, is the
quick print of the set for the music stand; it is not stored. Use the lesson handout for a lesson or rehearsal and Print setlist on stage. A setlist with more than 50 songs cannot get a
handout: the dialog says "A setlist handout can hold up to 50 songs."

**Notes on a ready handout** (from Advanced, on the library item):

- "A notation file could not be read": that sheet music file is damaged or unusual; its pages are replaced by a note.
- "A notation file was left out because the handout reached its size limit": a handout holds at most 600 pages of
  sheet music. Make a handout per song or leave out some sheet music.
- "The cover picture was left out because it is not a JPEG or is larger than 40 megapixels": the rest of the handout
  is complete.
- Sheet music that was scaled to fit the page, and characters the fonts cannot print, are listed too.

A handout that is too large to make at all (for example a very long setlist with every part) is refused before it
starts; leave out an ingredient or make fewer songs at once. "The handout could not be written" means making it took
too long or failed; press **Retry**.
