Many wind instruments read their music in another key than they sound. For the **Melodic instruments** handout choose
the transposition of your instrument:

- **C**: flute, oboe, violin, viola, cello, trombone and other instruments that read at concert pitch.
- **B-flat**: trumpet, clarinet, tenor sax and soprano sax (written a major second higher).
- **E-flat**: alto sax and baritone sax (written a major sixth higher).
- **F**: horn (written a perfect fifth higher).

The chords are transposed too, so the chord chart matches your part, and the facts say for example "Part in B-flat
(sounds a major second lower)". The handout uses the transposed part from the library when one exists; make one in the
sheet music dialog first.
