**Tolerance** is how far from the melody a note may be and still count, in cents (100 cents is a semitone). The default
is 100 cents at Beginner, 50 at Advanced and 25 at Expert; from Advanced you can set 10 to 200 cents. A change applies to
the next lines at once.

**Latency** is the delay between your voice and the song: the microphone and the speakers each add a little. Singing
feedback uses the latency calibrated in the recording settings ("calibrated"); without a calibration it uses an estimate
of the browser, which can make good lines score lower. Calibrate in the recording settings for exact timing.

At Expert a raw readout shows the pitch you sing in hertz, as a note and its deviation in cents.

Limits: the estimate can be off by tens of milliseconds on Bluetooth headphones; calibrate with the headphones you use.
