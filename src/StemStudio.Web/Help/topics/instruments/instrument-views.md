**Practise as ...** in the mixer is one entry point for every instrument family. Choose what you play and StemStudio
makes a **minus-one mix**: every channel of that family is muted - the stem and all its detail channels, also while
their strips are collapsed - and every other channel is unmuted and unsoloed; volumes and pans stay. Then the family's
view opens below the chords:

| Family | View |
|---|---|
| Vocals | Singing feedback: the pitch view and a score per line |
| Drums | Drum trainer: five hit lanes and the drum notation |
| Bass | Tablature: the bass part with tab, or *Generate tab* |
| Guitar | Tablature: the guitar part with tab, or *Generate tab* |
| Keys | Chords: the chord sheet with the lyrics |
| Strings / brass / woodwinds | Part for your instrument: choose your instrument and see or generate its part |

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Next time the mixer offers it again ("Practise as Keys again?") but does not
apply it until you click.

The practice presets *Play along* work the same way since this version: they mute the detail channels of the family
too, so an expanded drum group no longer stays audible.

Limits: the mute follows the separated stems; an instrument that the separation put into another stem (for example a
piano in *other*) is not muted with its family.
