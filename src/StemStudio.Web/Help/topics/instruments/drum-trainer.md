**Drumless** (in the mixer, one click) mutes the drums channel and every drum detail channel, also when the drum strips
are expanded. It switches the metronome on with a one-bar count-in and opens the drum trainer. Other channels are
unmuted and unsoloed so you hear the whole band; volumes and pans stay. A second click brings back your previous drum,
metronome and count-in settings.

The **drum trainer** shows five lanes, from top to bottom cymbals, hi-hat, toms, snare and kick. The hits scroll towards
the "now" line while the song plays; taller marks are harder hits. The hits of the bar you hear are also listed as text
for screen readers.

- **Split first**: the hits are found in the drum detail stems, so the drums must be split into their pieces. When they
  are not, the trainer says so and *Split the drums* starts the split; the hits are found right after. Drumless works
  without a split.
- **Guide piece** (from Advanced): click a lane label, for example *Hi-hat*, to keep that piece audible on top of the
  drumless mix. Click it again to mute it.
- **Look-ahead** (from Advanced): how far ahead the lanes show, 2 to 8 seconds (4 by default).
- **Drum notation**: when the song has drum sheet music, it is shown next to the lanes and follows the playback. Without
  it, *Generate drum notation* makes it.

Limits: hits are found from the split drum stems; fast fills, ghost notes and quiet hits can be missed, and bleed between
the pieces can add extra hits. With reduced motion the lanes page instead of scrolling.
