Every instrument on the song page and in the mixer has a **MIDI** button. It gives you the notes of that instrument
as a MIDI file that you can open in a music program.

- If sheet music of that instrument already exists, its MIDI file is downloaded at once.
- If not, StemStudio makes the sheet music first. You see the progress next to the button, and the download starts
  when it is ready.
- The notes are always at the pitch you hear (concert pitch), also for transposing instruments.
- Drums use the standard drum map on MIDI channel 10, so each drum piece plays the right sound in any drum program.

Limits: the notes are found by the computer, so quiet or fast passages can be incomplete. Sheet music for drums needs
the drums to be split first.
