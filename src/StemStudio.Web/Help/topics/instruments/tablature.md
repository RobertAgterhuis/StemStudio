Sheet music for the **guitar** and **bass** instruments can include tablature: one line per string, with the fret
number to play on it.

- **Layout**: *Notation and tab* shows the notes with the tab below (the default), *Tab only* shows just the tab and
  *No tab* leaves it out.
- **Tuning**: choose the tuning you play in. Beginner offers standard tuning and drop D; from Advanced you get every
  tuning, such as half a step down, DADGAD, open G, and 5- and 6-string bass.
- **Capo** (guitar, from Advanced): the fret numbers are counted from the capo. When capo mode is on, its capo is
  filled in for you.

Limits: notes that are too low or too high for the tuning are moved by an octave, and chord notes beyond the number of
strings are left out (the quietest first). The library shows how many notes were changed. The fingering is chosen by
the computer: it prefers small hand movements, but it can differ from how a guitarist would play the part.
