**Singing feedback** compares your voice with the lead vocal of the song while it plays.

1. The first time, the **reference melody** is analysed from the lead vocal detail stem (or the vocals stem when vocals
   were not split). Short songs start at once; for a long analysis the time it takes is shown first.
2. Choose your microphone in the recording settings (and calibrate the latency there for exact timing).
3. Press *Start singing feedback* and play the song. The lead vocal is muted while you sing and comes back when you stop.
   Use **headphones**, so the microphone hears only you and not the song.

The **pitch view** shows the melody as bars and your voice as a line. After every lyric line a score from 0 to 100
appears next to the karaoke banner and is announced to screen readers: the share of the melody you sang within the
tolerance. Singing an octave lower or higher counts the same, and a transposed song is scored in the new key. Without
synced lyrics, sections or blocks of bars are scored instead. When you stop, a summary shows your average and your best
line. A saved take of the recording panel can be scored the same way with *Score this take*.

Privacy: everything you sing stays in your browser: neither the microphone audio nor the scores are sent to the server.

Limits: the reference melody comes from the separated vocal stem, so bleed from other instruments can add wrong notes;
lines with very little melody get no score; on a slow device the pitch tracking stops itself with a message while the
song keeps playing.
