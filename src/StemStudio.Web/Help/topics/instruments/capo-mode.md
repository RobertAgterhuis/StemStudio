**Capo mode** is for guitarists: after *Practise as Guitar* it appears next to the chords in the mixer and on the track
page. With a capo on fret *n*, the chords are shown as the **shapes** you play (for example C, G, Am, F for a song in
E-flat with capo 3). The song sounds unchanged; a *Capo 3* badge reminds you where the capo goes.

- **Suggestions**: up to three capo positions (0 to 7) with the key of the shapes and how many of them are open chords,
  best first. One click applies a suggestion.
- **After a transpose**: when you transpose the song up (+1 to +7), *Capo n: play the original shapes* keeps the shapes
  of the original song; for -1 to -5 the capo 12 + transpose is offered when it is 7 or lower.
- **Sounding chords** (from Advanced): show the chord that sounds next to each shape, for example "C (Eb)".
- The chord lane, the karaoke banner, the chord sheet and its ChordPro download use the capo, and the tablature options
  of the sheet music dialog start with it. The capo is remembered per song in this browser.

Capo mode is offered for the guitar family only: bass and keys have no capo.

Limits: the suggestions count open-position shapes of the detected chords; barre chords, voicings and the real
difficulty of a song are not taken into account.
