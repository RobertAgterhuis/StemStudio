From the Advanced level you get more control over the chords.

- **Vocabulary**: *Simple* knows major and minor chords only, which is often the most readable choice. *Full* adds
  sevenths, sixths, diminished, augmented and suspended chords and inversions (a slash chord such as Am7/G). After a
  change, **Detect again** detects the chords with the new vocabulary.
- **Correct a chord**: on the track page, the pencil next to a chord in the chord sheet opens the editor. Choose the
  root, the quality and, for an inversion, the bass note. Corrections are kept; a later **Detect again** asks before it
  replaces them.
- **ChordPro**: the ChordPro link saves the chord sheet as a `.cho` text file with the transpose and the capo you
  use, for songbook apps.
- **Include chords** in the sheet music dialog puts the chord symbols above the top staff of a vocals, guitar, keys or
  piano score. It is offered once the chords are ready.
- Expert can choose the **method**: *Accurate* (the BTC model, Park et al. 2019, MIT licence; fetched once, about
  12 MB) or *Fast* (a chroma template match, works offline and on slow computers, less accurate).
