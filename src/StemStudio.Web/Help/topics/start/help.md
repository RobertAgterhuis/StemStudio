There are four kinds of help:

- **Tooltips**: hover or focus a control to see what it does, with a link to more.
- **The help panel**: the Help button, the rail item, ? or F1 open it at the topic of the focused control
  or of the current screen. Search it, follow links, or browse the index.
- **The glossary** explains the words used in StemStudio.
- **[Tours](help:tours)** walk you through a screen.

Escape closes the panel and returns you to where you were.
