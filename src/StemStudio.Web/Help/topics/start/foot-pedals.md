Most page-turner pedals send PageUp (left) and PageDown (right). Choose what they do under **Foot pedals**:

- **Play / Back to section start** (default): right pedal plays and pauses, left pedal goes back to the start of the part.
- **Next / previous section**, **Next / previous song (setlist)** or **Faster / slower**.
- **Custom**: give any command a pedal yourself under the commands above.

When your pedal sends other keys, press **Learn left pedal** or **Learn right pedal** and press the pedal.

**Pedal mode** lets the pedals work while a slider or button has the keyboard focus. In a text field they never do.
Holding a pedal down does not repeat the command. The test line below the setup shows what the last pedal press did.
