Open **Preferences** at the top right.

- **Theme**: System follows your computer's light or dark setting; Light and Dark override it.
- **Density**: Level default is compact at Expert and comfortable otherwise.
- **Motion**: Reduced turns off animations; System follows your computer.
- **Reset help and tours** shows the welcome panel, the tour offers and the hints again.

Preferences are stored in this browser. Another browser or clearing the site data starts with the defaults.
