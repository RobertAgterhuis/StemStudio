A tour highlights one part of the screen at a time and explains it. Start a tour from the offer on the
screen or from the list at the end of the help index.

- **Next** or the right arrow key moves on, **Back** or the left arrow key goes back.
- **Escape** or the close button ends the tour.

Once you finish or close a tour, its offer is not shown again until the tour is updated. Use
"Reset help and tours" in the preferences to see the offers again.
