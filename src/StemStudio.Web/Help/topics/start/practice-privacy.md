The practice log is stored only in StemStudio's own database on this computer. It is never sent to another computer
or service.

On the **Practice** page (side bar) you see the practice time per song. There you can:

- switch the **Practice log** off: from then on nothing is recorded, and the mixer says the log is off;
- **Delete the whole log** of every song;
- at Expert level see every session and delete a single one.

Deleting a song from your library also deletes its practice log. A backup of your library contains the log; a merged
restore keeps your current log.
