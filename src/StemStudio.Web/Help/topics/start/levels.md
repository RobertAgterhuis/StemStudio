The level switch at the top of every screen has three settings.

| Level | What you get |
|---|---|
| Beginner | The essential controls, the journey rail, practice presets and short hints |
| Advanced | Every control, as before |
| Expert | Advanced plus exact numbers, dB readouts, job details and a denser layout |

A level only changes controls and aids. It never hides your songs, your mixes, errors or the legal notice.
A value you set at a higher level stays applied at a lower one: a pan setting, for example, stays audible
and is shown as a small "Panned" note.
