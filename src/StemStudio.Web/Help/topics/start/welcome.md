StemStudio splits a song into separate instruments, called [stems](help:stem), so you can mute, solo and
mix them while you practise.

## Choose how you want to start

- **I am new to this** gives guided steps, practice presets and short hints.
- **I know my way around** shows all controls, as usual.
- **Give me everything** adds keyboard shortcuts, exact numbers and job details.

You can change the [level](help:levels) at any time with the level switch at the top of the screen.
