**Save to library** turns part of a song into an audio file you can play or download later.

## On the track page

Each stem and each kept detail stem has a save button. The stem is saved **raw**: exactly as separated, with no
volume, pan or mute applied. From the Advanced level **Save all stems** saves every stem as its own item.

## In the mixer

- **What I hear** saves exactly what you hear now: the audible channels with their volume, pan and the master
  volume, also when the mix is not saved. The metronome is never included.
- **Selected channels** (Advanced) saves the channels you tick, each with its volume and pan, regardless of mute
  and solo.

Choose a format and, optionally, a name; without a name StemStudio uses "<song> - <contents>". The file is
prepared in the background; **Open in library** shows it.

Exports always use the original speed and key, even while the mixer is slowed down or transposed for
[practice](help:practice-speed); the dialog says so then.
