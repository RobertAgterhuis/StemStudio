**Band info** on the track page and in the mixer opens a panel about the artist of the song.

## What you see

- At every level: the artist's name, type (group or person), active years, the album and year of the song and a
  short excerpt from Wikipedia.
- From the Advanced level also the country, genres, members (groups only), website and the list of sources.

The Wikipedia excerpt is shown unmodified with a link to the article, its authors and the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.
The facts come from MusicBrainz and Wikidata (CC0).

## Status

StemStudio looks the song up by itself after the import. While it searches, the panel shows a placeholder.
**No band information found** means there was no reliable match: nothing is guessed. **Search again** retries;
**Correct title and artist** searches with the names you enter, for example when a video title has no
"Artist - Title" form. When the services cannot be reached, the reason is shown and you can try again later.

When online lookups are switched off, see [Online lookups](help:online-lookups).
