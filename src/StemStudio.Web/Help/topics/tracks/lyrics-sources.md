After an import StemStudio asks **LRCLIB**, an open lyrics database, for the song's lyrics.
Only the title, artist, album and duration of the song are sent; no audio and nothing about you.
With online lookups switched off nothing is sent.

The lyrics card on the track page shows what was found:

- **Timed per word** or **per line**: the [karaoke banner](help:karaoke) in the mixer sings along. Line timing is spread
  over the words as an estimate.
- **Waiting for alignment**: only the text was found. It is [aligned to the vocals](help:lyrics-align) first.
- **Instrumental** or **Not found**: there is no banner. You can **enter the lyrics yourself**, one line per sung line.
- **Search again** asks LRCLIB once more. From the Advanced level **Remove** deletes the lyrics of this song; StemStudio
  then does not search for them again by itself.

Lyrics are copyrighted. StemStudio shows them for your personal practice only: they stay in the app and are never
exported, saved in mixes, logged or shared. The credit names the source.
