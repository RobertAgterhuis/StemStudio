- **Tempo** is the detected BPM, used by the metronome. "not detected" means no reliable beat was found.
- **Duration** is the length of the song.
- **Model** names the separation model and the number of stems it produced.

The coloured tabs list the stems. **Open mixer** takes you to the mixer.
