The **strength** decides how much of the original song the take keeps.

- **Subtle**: very close to the song; mostly the sound changes.
- **Light** and **Balanced**: the arrangement changes, the melody and the structure stay recognisable. Most styles
  start at Balanced.
- **Strong**: a clear new arrangement; details of the song may get lost.
- **Free**: the furthest away; the take may only loosely follow the song.

Each style has its own default strength; the slider starts there. Try two takes when you go further than Balanced.
