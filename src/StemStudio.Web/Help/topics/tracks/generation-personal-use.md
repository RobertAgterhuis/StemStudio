A restyled version of a song is still based on that song. Use generated takes for your own practice only:

- **Do not publish them.** The rights to the original song remain with its owners, as with every import.
- **No imitation.** Styles describe music (genre, instruments, mood, tempo). StemStudio refuses style texts that name an
  artist or ask to sound like one, and the curated styles never name artists, bands, songs or labels.
- **AI-generated.** Every take is labelled as such; keep that label when you use it.

You are responsible for what you do with the result, just as for the songs you import.
