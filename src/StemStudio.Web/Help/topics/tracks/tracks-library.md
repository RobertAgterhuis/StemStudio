The track library lists your imported songs, newest first. The coloured ribbon shows which stems a track has.

- Click a title to open the track page.
- **Open mixer** (Practise at Beginner) appears once the separation is complete.
- At Advanced and Expert you can search by title or artist and change the sort order.
