StemStudio shows one picture per song: large on the track page and in the mixer header, small in the track list.

## Where the picture comes from

The first available picture wins:

1. **Your own picture**, when you chose one.
2. The **Spotify album image** of a Spotify import.
3. The **cover embedded** in an uploaded MP3 or FLAC file.
4. The **album cover** of the matched album from the Cover Art Archive.
5. The **video thumbnail** of a YouTube import, cropped to a square.

When there is no picture, a coloured square with the song's initials is shown instead.

## Your own picture

From the Advanced level the track page has **Choose picture**. Pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP file of at most 10 MB.
It is cropped to a square and replaces the automatic picture everywhere. **Use automatic picture** goes back.

Pictures are shown in the app only and are never part of an export.
