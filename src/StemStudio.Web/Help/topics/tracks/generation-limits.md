**Generations** lists everything StemStudio made with AI, newest first. Each row shows its state (queued, waiting for the
graphics card, downloading the model, running, completed, failed or cancelled), the progress and, when it is done, its
takes.

- **Cancel** stops a generation that is queued or running.
- **Try again** starts a failed or cancelled generation again with new seeds.
- **Delete** removes the takes and their library items; tracks you imported from a take are kept.

What to expect:

- **The quality varies.** The same settings give different takes; make two and keep the best.
- **It needs a graphics card** with enough free memory. When another job uses it, the generation waits up to 10 minutes
  and then fails with the reason.
- **The artist check is not complete.** StemStudio refuses known imitation phrases and artist names from your library
  and from a curated list, but it cannot know every name. Never ask for an imitation of a real artist.
