StemStudio estimates the key from the notes and takes the bars from the song's [beat grid](help:beat-grid).

- **Simplified rhythm** writes eighth notes instead of sixteenths: easier to read, less exact. It is on by default
  for vocals.
- **Key** (Advanced): keep **Automatic** or choose one of the 30 keys, for example "E minor" or "B-flat major". The
  library shows the key of every score with how sure the estimate is.
- **Tempo**: when the beat grid of a song is not reliable (or a library item has no song), the dialog asks for the
  tempo in BPM (30 to 300), the beats per bar (1 to 12) and the time of the first downbeat in seconds. It fills in
  the detected tempo when there is one; check it with the metronome in the mixer. From the Advanced level you can
  also enter a tempo for songs with a reliable grid; leave the three fields empty to use the grid.
