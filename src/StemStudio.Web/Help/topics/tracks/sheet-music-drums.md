Drum notation is written from the drum detail stems: kick, snare, toms, hi-hat and cymbals, each on its own line of a
percussion staff, with hands and feet as two voices.

- If the drums of a song are not split yet, the dialog says so. **Split the drums** starts the detail separation
  (the same as **Split further** on the track page). Open sheet music again when the detail stems are ready.
- When a piece is missing (for example no toms were found), its line stays empty and the item shows a warning.
- Sound from one drum can leak into another detail stem and add extra hits; check busy passages by ear.

Single drum pieces have no sheet music button of their own: the drums profile always writes the whole kit.
