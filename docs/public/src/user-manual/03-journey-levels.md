# Experience levels

StemStudio adapts to how experienced you are. There are three levels: **Beginner**,
**Advanced** and **Expert**. Switch between them with the level switch in the top bar (on a
narrow screen it is in the preferences menu). Your choice is remembered in this browser.

A level only changes which controls and aids are shown. It never changes your data, your
saved mixes, error messages, limitation notes or the legal notice. The command palette and
keyboard shortcuts work at every level.

## Beginner

The default until you choose a level. Designed for your first sessions:

- A **journey rail** shows the four steps: Import → Separate → Mix → Save.
- **Practice presets** appear as large chips in the mixer (for example *Play along: drums*
  or *Karaoke*).
- Short hints appear next to important controls and tours are offered on each screen.
- The track list shows large cards; the main button on a track says **Practise**.
- Pan, typed loop times and metronome corrections are hidden. A channel that was panned in a
  saved mix shows a read-only **Panned** marker.

## Advanced

The complete set of practice controls:

- Pan per channel.
- Typed start and end times for the A-B loop.
- Metronome correction: BPM override, offset in milliseconds, beats per bar and reset.
- Search and sort in the track list and the mix library.
- Presets move into a compact menu.

## Expert

Everything of Advanced, plus tools for fast, precise work:

- Numeric fields for volume (0 to 100) and pan (−100 to 100) on every channel.
- Level meters with dB values.
- A **Job details** section on the track page (model, stage, progress, device and the reason
  for a CPU fallback).
- Extra columns (BPM and model) in the track list.
- Keyboard hints on buttons and a **Shortcuts** button in the top bar (the shortcut overview
  itself, <kbd>Mod</kbd>+<kbd>/</kbd>, works at every level).
- Compact density and the import link field focused as soon as the page opens.

## Preferences

The preferences menu in the top bar holds:

- **Theme**: System, Light or Dark. The dark "night session" theme is designed for dim
  rehearsal rooms; the light "day session" theme is the one used in this manual.
- **Density**: comfortable or compact (the level chooses a default).
- **Motion**: follow the system or always reduced.
- **Reset help and tours**: shows the welcome panel, hints and tour offers again.

Preferences are stored in your browser only. In a private window, where the browser does not
allow storage, StemStudio works at the Beginner level and shows a short notice.
