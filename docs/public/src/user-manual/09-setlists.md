# Setlists

A **setlist** is a list of songs in the order you play them: for a rehearsal, a gig or a practice
routine. You play it from the first song to the last, each song with the mix you chose for it, and
you can print it as a PDF for the music stand.

## Making a setlist

1. Open **Setlists** in the side bar.
2. Press **New setlist**, type a name (1 to 80 characters) and press **Create setlist**.
3. Press **Add songs**. Your finished songs are listed; pick one and, at the Advanced level, one of
   its saved mixes.

You can also press **Add to setlist** on a song page or next to a saved mix: that song (with that
mix) is added at the end of the setlist you choose. The same song may appear more than once, for
example as an encore. A setlist holds up to 100 songs.

## Editing a setlist

Every change is saved at once.

- Type a new name at the top to rename the setlist.
- **Move up** and **Move down** change the order without a mouse; you can also drag a song to its
  new place.
- **Remove** takes a song out of this setlist only; the song stays in your library.
- At the Advanced level each song can have its own mix and a short **note** (up to 1,000
  characters, for example *capo 2* or *start slow*), and the setlist a longer note (up to 2,000
  characters).

The total shows the number of songs and how long they last together. When you delete a song from
your library it also leaves your setlists; the setlist tells you once, for example *"1 song was
removed because the track was deleted."*

## Playing a setlist

**Play setlist** (or **Play** in the list of setlists) opens the first song in the mixer, with the
mix you chose for it. The bar above the mixer shows where you are (*song 2 of 9*) and the note of
the song.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Next song | **Next song**, <kbd>Mod</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd>, or the right pedal with the pedal preset *Next / previous song* (chapter 24) |
| Previous song | **Previous song**, <kbd>Mod</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd>, or the left pedal with that preset |
| Leave play mode | **Exit setlist** goes back to the setlist |

When you changed the mix of a song and did not save it, StemStudio asks before it moves on, as it
does when you leave the mixer.

## Print setlist

**Print setlist** makes an A4 PDF:

- first an **overview** of all songs with their artist, tempo, length and the start of your note,
  and the total length;
- then one page per song with its facts (artist, tempo and time signature, length, mix), your
  note, its **sections** (chapter 7) with their start times, and its **lyrics**, or *No lyrics*.

Every page has a footer with the setlist name, the page number and the date. At the Beginner level
the PDF comes with everything in it. At the Advanced level a dialog lets you leave out the lyrics,
the sections or the notes, and choose whether every song starts on a new page; then press
**Download PDF**.

Limits: lyrics longer than 10,000 characters are cut and marked *Lyrics shortened*. Characters
the PDF font cannot show (for example Chinese or Japanese script) are replaced, and the overview
says *Some characters could not be shown*. A setlist without songs cannot be printed: *"Add songs
first: an empty setlist has nothing to print."*

The PDF is made on your own computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

For a lesson or a rehearsal, **Lesson handout per instrument** makes a stored lesson PDF of the set for one
instrument family, with chords, sheet music and practice notes per song (chapter 23).

## Levels

| Level | What you see |
|---|---|
| Beginner | Setlists, new setlist, add, move, remove, play mode with next and previous, the PDF with everything |
| Advanced | Also a mix and a note per song, the setlist note and the PDF options |
| Expert | As Advanced |
