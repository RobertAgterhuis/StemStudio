# Score follows playback

When you made sheet music of a song (chapter 16), you can read it in the mixer while you play
along. The bar you hear is marked and the page scrolls with the music, at every practice speed.

## Opening the score panel

1. Make sheet music for a stem on the track page (chapter 16), for example the bass or the
   guitar part. It takes a moment after the sheet music is ready before it can follow the
   playback; the panel then says *Preparing the score view...*.
2. In the mixer press **Score** (<kbd>F</kbd>).
3. Choose the part in the list when you made more than one.

The list shows every sheet-music item made from this song, directly from a stem or from an
export of it, for every profile including drums and detail stems. Sheet music made from a library
item without a source track cannot follow a song and is not listed. Without sheet music the panel
says *No sheet music for this song yet* with a link to the song page.

## Following the bar

- The bar you hear is **outlined and hatched**, so you do not depend on colour. It follows the
  sound you hear: at every speed, in loops, after a seek and after a pause.
- The page **scrolls along** so the current line of music stays visible. When you scroll
  yourself, it waits 4 seconds and then follows again. Press **Follow** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>)
  to switch scrolling off or on. With reduced motion the page jumps instead of scrolling
  smoothly.
- **Click a bar** to play from there. At the Advanced level **Shift+click** loops the bars from
  the loop start (or just that bar) to the bar you clicked.
- **Read bar** tells a screen reader which bar is playing, for example *Bar 17 of 64*.

## Notes in the panel

| Note | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *The notation is shown in the original key; you hear it transposed.* | Transpose (chapter 5) is not 0. The notes on the page are not transposed |
| *This score was made with a tempo you typed, so the bars may drift from the music.* | The sheet music used a manual tempo because the beat grid was unreliable (chapter 16). Follow uses that tempo; for rubato songs the marked bar can run ahead or behind |
| *The score view could not be made.* | The page view of this sheet music failed; its PDF, MusicXML and MIDI in the library are not affected |

## Sheet music of a part of the song

Sheet music made from a range of the song, or from an exported loop, starts at the first bar of
that part. While the song plays before or after the part, no bar is marked; the highlight starts
on the score's first bar when the song reaches the start of the range.

## Levels

| Level | What you see |
|---|---|
| Beginner | The score panel, the choice of part, following on and off, click a bar |
| Advanced | Also Shift+click to loop bars |
| Expert | As Advanced |
