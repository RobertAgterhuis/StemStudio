# Practice log and privacy

While you play a song in the mixer, StemStudio notes how long you practise, so you can see that
you are getting better. The log is stored only on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

## What is logged

- The **playing time** of the song in the mixer: wall-clock time, with the count-in and without
  pauses. One minute at 50 % speed counts as one minute.
- The time **per section** (chapter 7) and how often a loop went round.
- The **tempo reached**: the highest speed of the speed trainer at which you played a whole
  section loop. StemStudio cannot hear whether you played cleanly, so every completed pass counts.
- The highest playback speed of each session.

A pause longer than ten minutes starts a new session. The log is saved every 30 seconds while you
play, so a crash or a power cut loses at most half a minute.

The first time the mixer logs, a short notice says *"StemStudio keeps a log of how long you
practise each song and section, only on this computer. It is never sent anywhere."* with **OK**
and **Turn the log off**.

## The practice log in the mixer

Press **Practice log** in the mixer to see it for this song:

- **Today**, **Last 7 days** and **Total**.
- A chart of the minutes per day.
- At the Advanced level a table with the time per section and the best trainer speed, and a chart
  of the trainer speed per section over time.
- **Show as table** shows the numbers of a chart as a table; a sentence below each chart says what
  it shows.
- **Delete the log of this song** removes its sessions.

## The Practice page

**Practice** in the side bar lists the practice time per song: total, last 7 days and when you
last practised, most recent first. There you can:

- switch the **Practice log** off: from then on nothing is recorded and the mixer says *"The
  practice log is off: nothing is recorded."* with **Turn on**. What was logged before stays until
  you delete it;
- **Delete the whole log** of every song;
- at the Expert level see every session and delete a single one.

## Privacy

- The practice log is stored only in StemStudio's own database on this computer. It is never sent
  to another computer or service.
- Deleting a song from your library also deletes its practice log.
- A backup of your library (chapter 22) contains the log; a restore that adds what is missing
  keeps your current log.

## Levels

| Level | What you see |
|---|---|
| Beginner | Today, last 7 days, total, minutes per day, the Practice page, switch off, delete |
| Advanced | Also the time and best trainer speed per section and the trainer chart |
| Expert | Also the list of sessions with delete per session |
