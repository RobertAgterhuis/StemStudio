# Legal notice

## Personal practice only

The import screen always shows this notice, at every experience level:

> "Imports are for personal practice only. You are responsible for complying with copyright
> and the platforms' terms of service."

StemStudio is a practice tool for one person on one computer. Import only songs you may use
for your own practice. You are responsible for complying with copyright law and with the
terms of service of YouTube and Spotify.

## No copy-protection bypass

StemStudio never circumvents DRM. For Spotify links it only reads the track information and
fetches a matching public recording from YouTube. Spotify support relies on unofficial
clients and may stop working.

## Stems, exports and generated audio

- Stems, mixes and library exports of copyrighted songs are for your personal practice. Do
  not publish or distribute them.
- Cover pictures, band information and lyrics are copyrighted by their owners. StemStudio
  shows them inside the app only and never exports them. Wikipedia excerpts are shown
  unmodified under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution.
- Style transfer and songs from lyrics produce **AI-generated** audio that is labelled in the
  app and inside the files. Restyled versions of copyrighted songs are not for publication.
  Styles may not imitate an artist.
- Voice conversion uses **only your own voice**, after your recorded consent, and only on
  this computer. You can delete your voice profile at any time.

## Optional paid AI service

The AI composer agent can optionally use Anthropic's Claude, which is paid per use. It is
off by default, only available when an API key is set in the environment, never switched on
automatically, and sends text only. Everything in StemStudio works without it.
